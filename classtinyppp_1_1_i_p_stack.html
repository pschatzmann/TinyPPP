Detailed Description

Minimal IPv4 stack (ICMP echo, UDP, TCP, DNS resolver) for platforms without lwIP.

The link layer (e.g. TinyPPP) derives from this class and implements the ip* methods. The socket methods are used by PPPClient, PPPServer and PPPUDP; sockets are identified by their index in the socket table.
